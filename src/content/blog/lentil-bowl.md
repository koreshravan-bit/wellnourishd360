This is a flexible template, not a strict recipe. Adjust portions to your appetite and needs.

## What you need
- 1 cup cooked lentils
- A handful of greens
- Chopped cucumber and tomato
- Olive oil, lemon, and herbs
- Optional: feta or tofu

## Put it together
Warm the lentils, add the vegetables and your choice of protein, then finish with lemon and olive oil. Serve with bread or rice if you’d like.
