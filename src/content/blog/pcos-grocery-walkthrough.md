There is no special aisle for PCOS. Stocking a few reliable staples makes it easier to put together satisfying meals.

## Start with familiar foods
Choose vegetables and fruit you enjoy, a few proteins such as eggs or beans, and carbohydrates you’ll actually eat. Frozen and canned options count.

## Put it together
Try a grain bowl with beans, vegetables, and a flavorful sauce, or a simple sandwich with a side of fruit. Consistency matters more than a perfect shopping trip.
