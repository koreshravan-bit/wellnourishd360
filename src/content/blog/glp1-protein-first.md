Appetite changes can make it hard to know what or how much to eat. Start with a few basics and adjust with your care team.

## Start with protein
Protein supports muscle and helps make smaller meals more nourishing. Your personal target depends on your health history, activity, and your clinician’s guidance. Try adding eggs, yogurt, tofu, fish, beans, or poultry to meals you already enjoy.

[key-takeaways]
Protein target | 1.0–1.5 g/kg/day
Protein per meal | 25–30 g
Fluids | 8+ cups/day
Strength training | 2–3×/week
[/key-takeaways]

## Build a small, balanced plate
If a full plate feels like too much, eat a smaller portion now and plan a snack later. Pair protein with produce and a carbohydrate you enjoy. There is no need to force a perfect meal.

## Make room for fluids
Sip water regularly. If nausea or constipation persists, tell your prescribing clinician. Your fluid needs may differ if you have heart or kidney conditions.

[inline-cta]

## Keep your care team in the loop
A dietitian can help you personalize these starting points. Talk to your prescriber before changing medications or supplements.
