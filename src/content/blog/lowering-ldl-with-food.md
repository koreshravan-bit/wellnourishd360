Your overall eating pattern matters more than any one superfood. Start with changes you can repeat most days.

## Add soluble fiber
Oats, beans, lentils, and some fruits provide soluble fiber. Try adding one of these to a meal you already make.

## Think about fats
Using olive oil, nuts, or seeds in place of some saturated-fat sources may support heart health. Your care team can help you decide what matters most for your labs.
