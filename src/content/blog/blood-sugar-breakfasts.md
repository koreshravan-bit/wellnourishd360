Breakfast doesn’t need to be complicated. Combining fiber-rich carbohydrates with protein and fat may help you feel satisfied and support more predictable glucose patterns.

## Five easy ideas
1. Greek yogurt with berries, nuts, and oats.
2. Eggs with whole-grain toast and sliced tomato.
3. Tofu scramble with peppers and a small tortilla.
4. Cottage cheese with fruit and seeds.
5. Overnight oats with chia and peanut butter.

## Notice your own patterns
Glucose responses vary. If you use a meter or CGM, notice which combinations work for you without judging a single reading.
