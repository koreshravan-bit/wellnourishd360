A kidney diagnosis does not automatically mean you need to avoid all potassium-rich foods. Your lab results, medications, and kidney function all matter.

## Begin with your labs
Ask your kidney care team whether your blood potassium is elevated and what range they recommend. Your dietitian can help translate those recommendations into meals.

## Keep variety on the table
Portion size and preparation can make a difference. Rather than cutting out whole food groups, plan a mix of fruits and vegetables suited to your current needs.
